## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏡 Roomora

#### Roommate Matching & Shared Living Platform

A platform for finding rooms, connecting compatible roommates, and managing shared living in Vietnam. Roomora brings the journey from room discovery and conversations to household management, shared expenses, and monthly reports into one application.

**Role:** Backend Developer

#### ⚙️ Tech Stack

**Backend:** `NestJS` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Redis` `SSE`

**Frontend:** `Next.js` `React` `Tailwind CSS`

**Integrations & Delivery:** `OpenAI` `Cloudinary` `Geoapify` `Google OAuth` `PayOS` `MoMo Sandbox` `Docker Compose` `GitHub Actions`

#### ✨ Key Features

- 🔐 **Authentication & Access Control** — Email OTP, Google sign-in, session management, and household-scoped permissions.
- 🏘️ **Room Listings & Discovery** — Drafts, photo uploads, moderation, location and budget filters, bookmarks, and maps.
- 🤝 **Explainable Roommate Matching** — Compare preferences and lifestyle habits with clear reasons behind compatibility scores.
- 💬 **Messaging & Appointments** — Text, images, voice messages, room-viewing schedules, and live updates through SSE.
- 🏠 **Household Management** — Member invitations, house leader permissions, announcements, group chat, and polls.
- 🧾 **Shared Expenses & Balances** — Record expenses, split contributions, track outstanding balances, and confirm payments.
- 📊 **Reports & Recurring Expenses** — Monthly spending reports, recurring expense drafts, and PDF exports.
- 🤖 **AI Assistance** — Editable listing descriptions, receipt-to-expense drafts, natural-language search filters, compatibility explanations, and spending summaries.
- 💳 **Service Payments & Refunds** — Listing promotion and AI credit packages, signed payment verification, duplicate callback handling, and refund workflows.
- 🛡️ **Admin & Support** — Listing moderation, user reports, support tickets, audit history, and estimated AI usage cost analytics.
- ⚡ **Shared Rate Limits & Caching** — Redis-backed request limits and map tile caching.
- 🧪 **Testing & Delivery** — Unit and HTTP integration tests, Docker packaging, CI checks, and container image publishing.

**Backend design:** Business modules use focused services with PostgreSQL transactions for data consistency. Roommate scores follow explicit rules; AI provides suggestions for review, while expense records and payment confirmation remain controlled by application workflows.
